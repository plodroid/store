How it works

Three moving parts. That's the whole system.

01

You edit in the app

Open the POS APK, change a price, drop a new product in, mark something sold out. Normal till work — nothing extra to learn.

02

Firestore takes the write

The app writes one document to the products collection. Works offline too — it queues and flushes when signal comes back.

03

The site redraws itself

A realtime listener is already open in every visitor's browser. No refresh, no rebuild, no deploy. The shelf just changes.
